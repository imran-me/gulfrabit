One layout, everyone. Publishing changes the home page for every visitor — there is no per-visitor setting.
A moving loop never stops on its own. It drifts leftward and speeds up while the page is being scrolled. Two of them stacked — the trust strip and the categories under it — is a lot of movement on one screen; it is allowed, because sometimes it is what you want, but look at it on a phone before you publish it.
A visitor who has asked their device to reduce motion gets none of the loops, automatically. They see a row they can swipe instead, and nothing is hidden from them.
No shape changes a price, a product or which products are on a shelf. That is the Home page screen.

What is not on this screen. The hero banners have their own, where how long each one is held, how it becomes the next and whether it advances at all are already settings — a second place to arrange the hero would be a second answer to the same question.
The newsletter band and the industry band have one honest arrangement each, so neither appears in the shape table below. When one of them grows a second, it will. The newsletter band can still be MOVED — where a section sits and what shape it wears are separate questions, and only the second one has run out of answers.

No backend connected. The shop is serving the layout built into its pages, and this screen cannot change that for visitors — a universal setting needs somewhere central to store it.
Preview still works, and it is safe: it only changes the shop in the tab it opens.
