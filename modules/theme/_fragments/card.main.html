Product cards

What each product card shows — and it can show less on a phone than on a computer. This is every card in the shop: the home page, the shop, a category, search, deals and the wishlist all draw the same one.

Part of the card On computers 768px and wider On phones under 768px
Wishlist heart Saves a product for later. Switching it off empties nobody’s wishlist — the account page still lists and manages it.
Quick-view eye Opens the product in a panel without leaving the list.
Discount badge The −47% chip. The struck-through old price stays either way.
Premium · New · B2B tags Not the stock badges. Sold out, Pre-order and Coming soon are always shown and cannot be switched off.
Brand line The vendor and origin above the title — “GulfRabit Select · Jordan”.
Rating stars Only ever on a product that has reviews; a product with none shows nothing here either way.
“You save ৳ 350” line Already hidden on phones, where the badge and the old price have said it twice — so the phone switch has nothing to change today.

One card, everyone. Publishing changes every product card for every visitor — there is no per-visitor setting.
What cannot be switched off, and why. The photograph, the title, the price, the size chips and the Add to Cart button. The first three are what a card is for, the chips are the control that decides which size goes in the basket, and the button is the sale. A switch that can empty a card of its purpose is a switch that will one day be left on by accident.
Switching a part off hides it, and changes nothing else. Prices, stock, wishlists and what is on which shelf are all somewhere else.

No backend connected. The shop is serving the card that was built into its pages, and this screen cannot change that for visitors — a universal setting needs somewhere central to store it.
