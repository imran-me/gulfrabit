How the storefront looks. Publishing takes effect for every visitor straight away — nothing is redeployed.

Storefront theme

One theme, everyone. Publishing changes the shop for every visitor — there is no per-visitor setting.
Luxe adds movement — a drifting background, a sheen across cards and buttons, rules that draw in as sections arrive. A visitor who has asked their device to reduce motion gets the colours and none of the movement, automatically.
No theme changes a price, a product or a page. Switching back is the same two clicks.

No backend connected. The shop is serving the theme that was built into its pages, and this screen cannot change that for visitors — a universal setting needs somewhere central to store it.
Preview still works, and it is safe: it only changes the shop in the tab it opens. To change it for everyone on a static deployment, rebuild with python tools/assemble.py --theme luxe and deploy.
