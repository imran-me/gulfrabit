Returns & refunds

We want you to be confident in every purchase.

  • Unopened, non-perishable items may be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Perishables, opened cosmetics, and cut-to-order industrial materials are non-returnable for safety reasons, unless faulty.
  • If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or with authenticity concerns, contact us within 48 hours for a replacement or full refund.

When your money comes back
